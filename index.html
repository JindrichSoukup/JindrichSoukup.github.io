Nálezník (Findings Herbarium)

App interface is in Czech.

A field-planning tool for iNaturalist's Česká flóra project: tell it where and when you'll be, and it shows which of your still-missing vascular plant species you could realistically find nearby, with real GPS localities, flowering windows, and warnings for species that are usually garden plants rather than wild ones. Built from iNaturalist, Pladias, and GBIF data, packaged as a single offline-capable HTML file — no install, no backend.

Magazine RAG (Živa archive pipeline)

I built this one by hand, layer by layer, to see what a retrieval system actually decides — and what it decides silently for you when you aren't looking. Two notes came out of it that outlast the code: a layer-by-layer map of every decision a RAG system makes, and what building it yourself says about build-vs-buy.

The pipeline turns 75 years of Živa, a Czech natural-history monthly, from one PDF per issue into a corpus you can query, cited down to the issue and page. The hard part is never the vector search; it is working out where one article ends and the next begins. Generalising it onto an unrelated magazine, The MagPi, surfaced five bugs the first one never could — and not one of them crashed anything.
