Nálezník (Findings Herbarium)

App interface is in Czech.

A field-planning tool for iNaturalist's Česká flóra project: tell it where and when you'll be, and it shows which of your still-missing vascular plant species you could realistically find nearby, with real GPS localities, flowering windows, and warnings for species that are usually garden plants rather than wild ones. Built from iNaturalist, Pladias, and GBIF data, packaged as a single offline-capable HTML file — no install, no backend.

Magazine RAG (Živa archive pipeline)

I built this one by hand, layer by layer, to see what building a retrieval system actually involves, then distilled what I learned into two notes: a layer-by-layer map of every decision such a system takes, and the build-vs-buy trade-offs behind them.

The pipeline turns 75 years of Živa, a Czech natural-history monthly, from one PDF per issue into a corpus you can query, cited down to the issue and page. The hard part is never the vector search; it is parsing and enriching the source. Živa's archive is under copyright, so the public demo runs on an unrelated, openly licensed magazine, The MagPi — porting it there took a handful of targeted fixes rather than a rewrite, which is the part current LLMs have genuinely made cheap.
